DELETE YOUR ACCOUNT

BRICKTOUCHER · BY SLOPCORE

A BrickToucher account only exists if you signed in to post scores to the online leaderboard. Here's how to delete it and everything stored with it.

IN THE APP (FASTEST)

  1. Open BrickToucher and tap LEADERBOARD on the title screen.
  2. Switch to WORLD. You'll see your name and rank at the bottom.
  3. Tap Delete my account, then tap it again to confirm.
  4. If you signed in with Apple, confirm with Apple once more. This lets us revoke BrickToucher's access to your Apple ID too.

Your account is deleted straight away and you're signed out. If you've signed in with both Apple and Google, they're separate accounts: sign in with each and delete it.

BY EMAIL

If you no longer have the app, email apps@slopcore.io with the subject "Account deletion" and tell us:

  • your leaderboard name,
  • whether you signed in with Apple or Google, and
  • roughly your best score, so we can tell it's yours.

We don't keep email addresses, so we can't look you up by yours. We'll delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • your sign-in record, which holds only your Apple or Google account identifier;
  • your leaderboard name;
  • your scores, levels, lines and game counts in every mode.

What isn't

  • scores, settings and local bests stored on your phone. Uninstalling the app removes them;
  • your SURVIVAL purchase, which belongs to your App Store or Google Play account, not to us. It stays yours;
  • short-lived server request logs, which expire on their own.

More detail is in the privacy policy.
