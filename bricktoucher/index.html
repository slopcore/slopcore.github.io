HOW IT WORKS

LINES

Falling blocks, full rows clear. Fill a row in a single colour for a PRISM ×3.

SQUARES

Every block is one of two colours. Match a 2×2 and the sweep line wipes it on the beat.

COMBOS

Keep the sweep erasing squares pass after pass to multiply your line score.

BEATS

Each level is a new genre of generated music, from chillhop through house and garage to drum & bass.

I bought FULL but it's locked

Open BrickToucher while you're online and signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account you bought it with. The game checks your purchase with the store each time it starts. If it's still locked, email us.

Do I need an account?

No. Signing in (with Apple or Google) is only needed to post scores to the online leaderboard. Everything else works without it.

How do I change or remove my leaderboard entry?

In the app, open LEADERBOARD → WORLD: you can rename yourself there, or tap Delete my account to remove your entry and scores. You can also ask us by email.

How do I report an offensive leaderboard name?

Names go through a profanity filter before they're accepted, but if one gets past it, email apps@slopcore.io with the name and the board it's on (ARCADE or SURVIVAL). We'll review it and remove the name, or the account behind it, within 24 hours.

What data does BrickToucher collect?

Nothing unless you sign in to the leaderboard, and then only what it needs. No ads, analytics or tracking. See the privacy policy.

How do I turn the sound or vibration off?

Pause the game with the button at the top right. The pause screen has switches for music, sound effects and vibration.
